Do not record sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the
agreed purpose.

## The real decision

- What decision was being made?
- Why did it matter?
- When did it need to be made?
- Who held formal authority?
- Was that authority clear to participants?
- Who was affected by the outcome?
- Whose knowledge or interests needed to be represented?

## What happened

### Before the decision

- How was the question framed?
- How were participants chosen?
- Where did evidence and preparation live?
- What was already decided before participation began?

### During the decision

- How were options, uncertainty, and disagreement handled?
- Who could influence the process, and who could not?
- What became difficult or burdensome?

### After the decision

- How was the judgement explained?
- What commitments or unresolved questions followed?
- What later had to be reconstructed?
- What, if anything, was reused?

## Existing response

- What tools, meetings, consultants, or workarounds were used?
- What worked well?
- What failed, duplicated work, or created friction?
- What was the cost in time, trust, delay, or avoidable risk?

## Strongest evidence

Record the participant's strongest statement about the problem. Mark it as
`verbatim` only when the wording is exact and attribution is permitted;
otherwise mark it `paraphrase`.
