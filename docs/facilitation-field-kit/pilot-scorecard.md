## Burden and harm check

- Where did the process consume more time than expected?
- Who found participation difficult or inaccessible, and why?
- Did anyone misunderstand how their contribution would be used?
- Were there confidentiality, safeguarding, conflict, or trust incidents?
- What should not be repeated?

## Outcome

- Was an attributable decision made? yes / no / not yet
- If no, was the decision not to proceed explicit and justified?
- Were unresolved questions preserved?
- Which output or practice was reused?
- What evidence supports continuing, changing, or stopping the offer?
