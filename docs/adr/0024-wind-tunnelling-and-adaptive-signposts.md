# ADR 0024 — Wind-tunnelling and adaptive signposts remain advisory

## Status

Accepted for Phase 13.

## Context

Scenario work is useful only when it changes how teams examine options and monitor uncertainty. Automatically ranking strategies or declaring one scenario to be emerging would create false precision and weaken human accountability.

## Decision

Wind-tunnel assessments pair one scenario with one active option from the scenario set's linked decision. They record an explicit verdict, desirability, feasibility, resilience, rationale, conditions, vulnerabilities, and mitigations. The calculated robustness score is descriptive and never selects an option or advances a decision.

Adaptive signposts are owned, observable indicators with thresholds, direction, cadence, and explicit relationships to one or more scenarios. Dated observations may be linked to existing sources. Observations remain evidence for human interpretation and do not automatically activate scenarios or organisational actions.

## Consequences

- teams can identify robust, conditional, vulnerable, and infeasible options across worlds;
- strategic assumptions become monitorable rather than static prose;
- attribution and disagreement remain visible;
- decisions retain their existing authority and lifecycle controls;
- future alerting can be added without turning signposts into autonomous decision rules.
