# ADR 0014: In-app notifications and explainable PostgreSQL analytics

## Status

Accepted for Phase 6.

## Context

Users need to know when accountable work changes, and organisation leaders need a small amount of decision-system feedback. A pre-revenue product should not require a push-notification vendor, data warehouse, behavioural-tracking SDK, or dashboard platform.

## Decision

Notifications are private tenant-scoped PostgreSQL records addressed to one user. Core workflow services create them explicitly after authority and business-rule checks. A deduplication key prevents repeated delivery of the same event. Due outcome reviews can be generated by an optional Celery Beat schedule or a management command; the underlying workflow does not depend on the scheduler.

Organisation analytics are read models computed from existing PostgreSQL records. Phase 6 exposes only measures with explicit definitions:

- decision counts and lifecycle distribution;
- decisions created and finalised in the last 90 days;
- median calendar days from creation to human finalisation;
- overdue target dates;
- open-decision participant coverage;
- completed outcome reviews and recorded outcome assessment distribution;
- due outcome reviews and active lessons.

## Consequences

- No external analytics or notification service receives customer data.
- Metrics remain explainable and cannot be presented as an automated quality score.
- A future materialised read model can be introduced if measured query cost justifies it without changing domain records.
- Email or push delivery can later subscribe to durable notification records, but it is not required for Phase 6 operation.
