# ADR 0022 — Traceable strategic implications

## Status

Accepted for Phase 12.

## Context

Foresight creates value only when its interpretation can influence accountable organisational choices. A separate foresight repository would force users to copy conclusions manually into decision documents and break provenance.

## Decision

A strategic implication belongs to one foresight canvas, may reference multiple drivers, has a human owner and priority, and may link to one decision in the same organisation. Linked implications are displayed in the decision overview and included in decision dossiers.

The implication remains advisory. Linking it does not modify the decision lifecycle, create an option, or select an outcome.

## Consequences

- decision authorities can see why a future-facing issue is relevant;
- foresight and decision records remain independently governed but traceable;
- customer exports preserve the complete source-to-signal-to-driver-to-implication-to-decision chain;
- scenario testing can later attach scenario-specific implications without redesigning decisions.
