# CrowdSmarter simulated client organisations

The seed command creates three **fictional** organisations. They are not representations of, subsidiaries of, or endorsements by any real organisation.

## NorthStar Grid Services

An electricity-network simulation inspired by common energy-transition decision patterns. It demonstrates:

- signal monitoring and source credibility;
- system drivers and stakeholders;
- four critical-uncertainty scenarios;
- wind-tunnelling three investment options;
- collective scoring and a minority report;
- a decision at the ready-for-decision stage.

## CareWeave Regional Health

A regional-health simulation inspired by common service-transformation decisions. It demonstrates:

- patient, workforce, clinical, and operational perspectives;
- inclusive contribution requests and review;
- digital-exclusion and safety risks;
- an open-for-contribution decision;
- scenario testing of a virtual-care operating model.

## TerraFood Futures Institute

An agri-food research simulation inspired by common long-horizon research-portfolio choices. It demonstrates:

- technology and policy foresight;
- research-programme option comparison;
- finalisation and implementation commitment;
- decision review and organisational lesson capture.

## Data safety

- Every organisation description contains `[SIMULATED CLIENT — NOT A REAL ORGANISATION]`.
- All named people are fictional.
- All fictional user accounts have unusable passwords.
- Existing active administrators receive access to the simulated organisations.
- The command is additive and transactional.
- It refuses to overwrite an existing simulation.
