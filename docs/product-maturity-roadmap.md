# Product maturity roadmap

CrowdSmarter is being developed as a foresight-to-decision-to-learning operating system. Maturity is measured by complete, trustworthy customer workflows rather than by feature count.

## Delivered foundations: Phases 1–10

The first ten phases established tenant-safe identity, governed decision lifecycles, options, evidence, assumptions, risks, stakeholder positions, human finalisation, implementation and outcome learning, advisory AI, collaboration, guided creation, professional interaction, account recovery, notifications, search, analytics, and customer-controlled exports.

## Phase 11 — Evidence, sources, and signal intelligence

Delivered:

- attributable source records and source-quality assessment;
- private, validated attachments using Django storage;
- local development storage and an S3-compatible production path;
- manual, bounded RSS and Atom ingestion;
- human-interpreted STEEP signals, horizons, maturity, impact, and uncertainty;
- accountable watchlists and signal review;
- signal-to-decision and source-to-evidence traceability;
- search, radar views, exports, audit, and offboarding protection.

## Phase 12 — Foresight canvas and systems mapping

Delivered:

- governed foresight canvases;
- trends, drivers, predetermined elements, critical uncertainties, and wild cards;
- signal-to-driver provenance;
- stakeholder mapping;
- causal relationships and explicit feedback loops;
- Futures Wheels and Three Horizons;
- strategic implications and leverage points linked to decisions;
- tenant-safe search, export, audit, and ownership rules.

## Phase 13 — Scenario intelligence and adaptive signposts

Delivered:

- two-axis scenario sets based on active critical uncertainties;
- four governed scenario worlds with narratives and assumptions;
- driver states, opportunities, threats, and implications;
- collaborative scenario reviews;
- wind-tunnelling of live decision options;
- transparent robustness, vulnerability, and mitigation records;
- adaptive signposts and sourced observations;
- decision-dossier, search, audit, and offboarding integration.

## Phase 14 — Collective evaluation and prioritisation

Delivered:

- independent and optionally peer-anonymous input;
- server-enforced blind results;
- scorecards, approval voting, consent, and Delphi rounds;
- quorum, thresholds, confidence, weighting, and sensitivity analysis;
- preserved minority reports;
- constrained organisation-level prioritisation under budget and capacity;
- explainable recommendations kept separate from accountable authority selections;
- search, exports, audit, tenant isolation, and ownership-safe offboarding.

## Phase 15 — Integrated decision analysis and executive synthesis

Delivered in this release:

- one option-centred read model over evidence, assumptions, risks, positions, scenarios, foresight links, collective evaluations, and minority reports;
- transparent evidence balance and source-quality indicators;
- assumption and risk review warnings;
- scenario robustness and collective-evaluation summaries;
- governed contradiction, gap, objection, vulnerability, resource, and implementation issues;
- assigned ownership, due dates, resolution attribution, audit, and offboarding protection;
- versioned human decision-quality reviews without a universal quality score;
- versioned, structured, human-approved executive summaries;
- protected synthesis drafts visible only to accountable authorities;
- search and customer-export integration;
- no option selection, lifecycle transition, or automated authority in the analysis layer.

## Delivered stabilisation: Phase 15.1 — Public experience and access reliability

Delivered:

- a professional public narrative and product preview;
- a persistent, privacy-conscious, rate-limited request-demo workflow;
- explicit case-insensitive email authentication;
- secure local first-run and password-repair commands without a production default credential.

## Phase 16 — Contribution orchestration

Delivered in this release:

- structured, decision-scoped contribution requests;
- named assignees, optional reviewers, priorities, due dates, and controlled pre-submission reassignment;
- evidence, assumption, risk, stakeholder, option, question, review, scenario, and implementation assignments;
- private saved drafts and immutable sequenced submissions;
- explicit submitted, under-review, accepted, returned, and cancelled states;
- append-only reviews and return guidance;
- facilitated workshop sessions and attendance records;
- cross-organisation assignee and reviewer inbox;
- due reminders and optional immediate, daily, or weekly email delivery;
- descriptive representation and participation coverage;
- search, exports, notifications, audit, tenant isolation, and offboarding protection;
- no general task board, chat system, employee scoring, or automated work allocation.

## Phase 17 — Organisation methodology and administration

Delivered in this release:

- organisation-owned decision methods created from a clean frame or cloned from built-ins;
- draft, approved, and retired governance with immutable approved versions;
- required framing, evidence, risk, assumption, stakeholder, checklist, and lifecycle prompts;
- exact method-version provenance on decisions and customer exports;
- organisation profile, brand identity, invitation policy, and default invitation role;
- append-only membership and ownership history;
- explicit ownership transfer;
- owner-controlled retention waiting period;
- controlled deactivation and delayed deletion requests;
- tenant-safe search, audit, permissions, and local-access provisioning;
- no preferred option, automatic outcome, or lifecycle authority in a method.

## Phase 18A — Accessibility remediation foundation

Delivered in this release:

- route-specific titles, polite route announcements, and post-navigation focus management;
- one-main-landmark discipline and reliable skip navigation;
- accessible workflow tabs and quick-navigation dialog behaviour;
- field-error announcements and clear not-found recovery;
- stronger focus visibility, target sizing, reduced-motion, increased-contrast, and forced-colours behaviour;
- automated public-route, keyboard, form-label, focus-restoration, and landmark regression checks;
- a frontend-only atomic installation path with no database operations.

## Phase 18B — Platform administration and tenant governance

Delivered in this release:

- explicit product-level platform-administrator capability separate from Django technical flags;
- protected platform operations workspace for users, organisations, decision enquiries, contacts, and audit;
- reasoned, expiring read-only or operational tenant support access;
- ownership, invitation, account-state, and organisation-state safeguards;
- automatic membership only in clearly marked fictional demo organisations;
- no silent platform-admin membership or contribution identity in real client tenants.

## Recommended next sequence

### Evidence-led delivery rule

The current development basis is structured internet research, documented in
`docs/research/internet-research-method.md`. The September 2026 baseline keeps
facilitation ahead of software and directs CrowdSmarter away from a general
participation portal, whiteboard, meeting tool, survey product, specialist
grant-management suite, or proprietary global signals library.

Before a new application capability enters the roadmap, it must clear the
research threshold, have a direct role in the facilitated process, and be a
better choice than integration, export, or a facilitation template. Adoption,
effectiveness, and willingness to pay remain unknown without behavioural
evidence and must not be treated as demonstrated.

### Phase 18C — Security headers and observability (local foundation delivered)

Delivered: CSP, permissions and cross-origin browser headers; proxy-to-Django
request identifiers; secure-cookie, CSRF, rate-limit and dependency checks;
liveness/readiness endpoints; structured JSON request logs; bounded Prometheus
metrics; and a locally exercised Loki/Alloy/Prometheus/Grafana/Alertmanager
profile with a dashboard and baseline alerts. Production still needs a tested
external receiver, infrastructure/error monitoring, multiprocess-aware
metrics, and an independent review against the deployed origin.

### Phase 18D — Upload protection and operational assurance (local foundation delivered)

Delivered: fail-closed ClamAV scanning, clean-only download/export, legacy-file
rescanning, a successful isolated restore drill, a non-destructive retention
review job, production email checks, operational runbooks, and an internal
security/privacy review. Remaining: owner/legal policy approval, legal-hold and
two-person deletion execution, production email/alert evidence, an independent
review, and representative browser/assistive-technology verification before
any conformance claim.

### Phase 19 — Hybrid facilitation and research operations

Delivered in this update:

- an internet-research method, reusable claim ledger, and dated baseline;
- a hybrid session capture pack covering influence, channels, source capture,
  synthesis, authority response, and process review;
- structured session briefs that state what participants can influence, what
  is fixed, which participation channels are used, and which perspectives are
  still missing;
- provenance-aware records for in-person, telephone, paper, partner-assisted,
  and digital input, separating participant input from facilitator synthesis;
- anonymous, confidential, and attributed capture with quote permission and
  accountable follow-up ownership;
- an authority feedback loop recording what was heard, what changed, what did
  not change and why, and what happens next; published responses are immutable;
- privacy-redacted, print-ready HTML session reports in the application and
  decision dossier, suitable for browser Save as PDF.

Remaining:

- a maintained research cadence with dated successor decisions;
- low-bandwidth and small-screen verification of critical participation and
  facilitation journeys;
- offline capture import for sessions that cannot reliably reach the platform;
- common imports and client-owned exports before bespoke integrations;
- guided organisation onboarding and a sample facilitation-to-decision
  workspace;
- contextual help, security and privacy documentation, support workflows, and
  privacy-conscious operational diagnostics.

### Phase 20 — Adaptive strategy and forecasting

Only after a new evidence review clears the work:

- signpost-triggered contingent actions;
- review, expiry, reversal, acceleration, and stopping conditions;
- visioning, backcasting, and adaptive roadmaps;
- no-regret, hedging, contingent, and reversible action portfolios;
- resolvable forecast questions, probability revisions, aggregation, resolution, and calibration.

## Deliberate non-goals before demonstrated demand

CrowdSmarter should integrate with rather than reproduce advanced financial modelling, GIS, specialist simulation and statistics, enterprise document editing, video conferencing, general-purpose team chat, and licensed global news databases. It remains the workflow and intelligence spine carrying external inputs through anticipation, collective judgement, accountable action, and learning.
