> **Superseded planning record:** active development is now based on the
> structured internet-research method in `docs/research/`, not on an outreach
> campaign. The organisations below are retained as historical research leads
> and should not be contacted merely because they appear in this file.

## Purpose and caution

These organisations are **research-qualified prospects**, not confirmed buyers or existing CrowdSmarter clients. They were selected because their public strategies or programmes show a current need for one or more of the capabilities CrowdSmarter provides: strategic foresight, stakeholder participation, evidence traceability, scenario testing, collective evaluation, decision governance, implementation review, and organisational learning.

The recommended entry point is a bounded pilot around one live decision. Do not lead with a large enterprise-platform sale. Lead with a decision audit, a facilitated pilot, and a documented before/after evidence trail.

## Priority 1 — strongest early-pilot prospects

### 1. National Economic and Social Council (NESC)

**Why it fits:** NESC explicitly describes horizon scanning, engagement with stakeholder groups, innovative methods, and exploration of future scenarios as part of its foresight work.

**Likely buying centre:** Secretariat leadership; foresight and policy-analysis programme leads; societal-engagement workstream.

**Pilot:** Run one cross-stakeholder policy decision from signal capture through scenarios, minority reports, decision conditions, and signposts.

**Entry message:** “CrowdSmarter can preserve the chain from horizon scanning and stakeholder input to the final policy judgement, including unresolved disagreement and the evidence that should trigger reconsideration.”

**Public evidence:**
- https://www.nesc.ie/work-programme/facilitate-and-support-policy-making-and-societal-engagement-strategic-outcome-three/
- https://www.nesc.ie/work-programme/reframe-refresh-improve-current-policy-options-strategic-outcome-one/

### 2. Technological University Dublin — Research & Innovation

**Why it fits:** TU Dublin publishes an engagement-and-impact pathway spanning early planning, stakeholder engagement, evaluation, and communication of outcomes. Its research strategy also prioritises external engagement and real-world impact.

**Likely buying centre:** Vice-President for Research and Innovation; Research Engagement and Impact; research-support and challenge-programme leads.

**Pilot:** Prioritise a small portfolio of research or sustainability projects using transparent criteria, stakeholder perspectives, scenario robustness, and outcome commitments.

**Entry message:** “CrowdSmarter can turn engagement and impact planning into an inspectable decision record rather than a collection of workshop outputs and spreadsheets.”

**Public evidence:**

### 3. Teagasc

**Why it fits:** Teagasc has an established technology-foresight practice and describes long-horizon evidence for policy and future science and technology programmes. Its strategy also calls for stronger stakeholder engagement across state agencies, farming organisations, industry, research organisations, and NGOs.

**Likely buying centre:** Research Directorate; Technology Foresight; programme and portfolio leadership; stakeholder-engagement strategy owners.

**Pilot:** Select or review one 2035 research programme using signals, systems maps, scenarios, farmer and industry contributions, multi-criteria evaluation, and learning gates.

**Entry message:** “CrowdSmarter can connect foresight outputs directly to research-portfolio choices and retain the rationale, uncertainties, dissent, and review signposts.”

**Public evidence:**

**Why it fits:** LGMA connects all 31 local authorities, supports strategy and policy development, and manages innovation and reform across planning, housing, water, waste, and environmental services.

**Pilot:** Compare and prioritise local-authority interventions across several councils while preserving local evidence, minority concerns, capacity constraints, and shared review criteria.

**Entry message:** “CrowdSmarter can support a common decision method across councils without erasing local context or forcing artificial consensus.”

## Historical outreach order

1. Start with warm-access organisations or units: TU Dublin, Trinity, NESC, and Teagasc.
2. Offer a 60–90 minute decision audit using one real but non-confidential decision.
3. Convert the strongest opportunity into a four-to-six-week paid pilot.
4. Use the pilot to create a decision-quality baseline, an attributable evidence chain, and an executive output.
5. Approach LGMA, Dublin City Council, SEAI, EPA, ESB Networks, and HSE with a validated case study rather than a generic platform demonstration.

## First pilot package

**Name:** Foresight-to-Decision Pilot

**Duration:** Four to six weeks

**Boundaries:** One organisation, one decision, up to twelve participants, up to four scenarios, and one executive decision record.

**Outputs:**
- decision framing and scope;
- stakeholder and contribution map;
- evidence, assumption, and risk register;
- signal watchlist and scenario set;
- transparent option evaluation and minority report;
- executive decision summary;
- implementation conditions and signposts;
- retrospective and reusable method.

**Commercial principle:** Charge for a facilitated decision outcome, not for software seats alone. The software becomes the durable governance layer around the engagement.
