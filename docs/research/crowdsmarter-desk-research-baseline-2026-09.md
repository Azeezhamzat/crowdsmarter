# CrowdSmarter internet-research baseline

**Date:** 2 September 2026  
**Status:** internal product and facilitation decision record  
**Method:** `docs/research/internet-research-method.md`

## Decision

Continue CrowdSmarter as a facilitation-led practice with a supporting
decision record. Do not expand it into a general community portal, meeting
tool, survey product, whiteboard, grant-management suite, or proprietary news
and foresight database.

The evidence supports a real need for well-designed participation, inclusive
facilitation, accountable use of contributions, and feedback after decisions.
It does not yet demonstrate demand for CrowdSmarter itself, product adoption,
effectiveness in use, or willingness to pay. Those claims remain unknown and
must not appear in public copy.

## Priority-context reading

### Morocco

The strongest current signal is not a request for another participation
website. PACTO already has a national digital space. The more relevant signals
are the stated need to institutionalise openness across many local
authorities, combine in-person and digital contributions, train facilitators,
co-create programmes, monitor implementation, and share learning. This points
toward facilitation design, repeatable records, accountable responses, and
cross-cycle learning.

Morocco's high mobile and internet use makes lightweight digital support
plausible, while the urban-rural and fixed-access gaps make mobile-first and
assisted participation necessary. The conclusion is hybrid delivery, not a
digital-only service.

### Nigeria

Nigeria's 2023–2025 Open Government Partnership commitments describe
participation across budgets, service delivery, local assemblies, town halls,
peace and security forums, traditional media, and digital channels. They also
identify weak feedback and trust. Their independent results remain pending,
so this supports the relevance of process design and a record connecting
input, authority, resolutions, response, and implementation, but not a claim
that the planned mechanisms succeeded.

Connectivity constraints are material enough that the platform should not be
the primary participation boundary. A facilitator must be able to gather
input through meetings, phone, paper, radio-linked processes, or partner
channels and enter only the necessary structured record later.

These country readings guide internal priorities. They are not claims about
named customers and do not belong in public platform copy.

## Product decisions from this baseline

### Build now

1. A research ledger and dated evidence-to-roadmap decisions.
2. A hybrid facilitation pack that works without requiring participants to use
   the platform.
3. A clear facilitator capture boundary for attributed and unattributed input,
   disagreement, missing perspectives, accessibility needs, and consent.
4. An explicit response-and-feedback-loop output that says what was heard,
   what changed, what did not change and why, who decided, and what happens
   next.
5. Low-bandwidth and small-screen verification of the critical participant and
   facilitator journeys before adding more modules.

### Integrate or export

- video conferencing, calendars, email delivery, documents, whiteboards,
  surveys, GIS, specialist voting, public engagement portals, nonprofit
  verification, payment operations, and licensed signal libraries;
- common, inspectable interchange formats before bespoke integrations;
- client-owned exports as the default hand-off boundary.

### Defer

- Arabic or French product exposure until the full user journey, operational
  emails, accessibility behaviour, support content, and review capacity can be
  delivered together;
- automated transcription or AI synthesis of sensitive participation until
  consent, source fidelity, correction, retention, and human approval are
  designed and tested;
- additional strategy, foresight, voting, grantmaking, or analytics modules
  unless a new evidence review clears the threshold in the research method;
- pricing and packaging conclusions, because this research does not establish
  willingness to pay.

### Avoid

- claims that participation automatically creates consensus, trust, better
  decisions, or improved services;
- opaque participant scores, automated final decisions, or AI-written
  consensus that erases minority positions;
- platform-only participation where non-digital or assisted channels are
  needed;
- a public narrative that presents the platform as all of CrowdSmarter.

## Evidence gaps

Internet research has not answered:

- which specific organisations will authorise or fund an engagement;
- which decision type provides the best initial entry point;
- the real burden on facilitators and participants using CrowdSmarter;
- whether the platform reduces reconstruction work in practice;
- whether organisations will reuse the process;
- price, procurement path, sales cycle, support burden, or retention;
- comparative outcomes against a skilled facilitator using ordinary tools.

Development may continue without outreach, but these gaps must remain marked
as unknown. Public effectiveness, adoption, or commercial claims require
behavioural evidence if that becomes feasible later.

## Review trigger

Refresh this baseline before any large application module, public entry into a
new jurisdiction, language launch, production processing of sensitive
participation data, or claim about outcomes. Record changes in a dated
successor rather than silently rewriting this baseline.
