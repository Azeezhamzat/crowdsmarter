## Decision note

For every claim that produces a build or integration decision, record:

- the user or facilitation problem;
- the smallest reversible response;
- the specialist alternative considered;
- the privacy, security, accessibility, language, and operational boundary;
- the expected observable result;
- what will be removed or changed if the result is not supported;
- the date and person responsible for the next review.

Use the scoring and claim-state definitions in
`docs/research/internet-research-method.md`. Do not convert the total into a
claim of product-market fit, effectiveness, or legal compliance.
