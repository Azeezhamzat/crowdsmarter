# Phase 13 release notes — scenario intelligence and adaptive strategy

Phase 13 turns the structured drivers and critical uncertainties from Phase 12 into governed scenario exercises. It helps teams test strategic options across multiple plausible worlds without presenting forecasts as facts or allowing AI to choose a preferred future.

## Customer capabilities

- create a bounded 2×2 scenario set from two distinct, active critical uncertainties on one foresight canvas;
- define meaningful low and high endpoint labels for each uncertainty rather than relying on abstract axes;
- construct up to four distinct scenario worlds, one per quadrant, with a short code, headline, narrative, assumptions, opportunities, and threats;
- describe how any canvas driver strengthens, weakens, stabilises, becomes volatile, transforms, or remains uncertain in each scenario;
- link scenario-specific effects back to the strategic implications already governed in the Phase 12 canvas;
- collect one structured review per organisation member covering plausibility, internal consistency, distinctiveness, usefulness, confidence, and qualitative comment;
- preserve visible confidence dispersion rather than collapsing disagreement into silent consensus;
- link a scenario set to one live decision and wind-tunnel only the active options from that decision;
- assess option desirability, feasibility, resilience, verdict, conditions for success, vulnerabilities, and mitigations in each scenario;
- define adaptive signposts with observable indicators, thresholds, direction, review cadence, ownership, and explicit relationships to scenario worlds;
- record dated observations against signposts and optionally ground them in existing organisation sources;
- retrieve scenario sets, scenarios, and signposts through tenant-safe PostgreSQL search;
- include all Phase 13 records in organisation exports and linked scenario evidence in decision dossiers;
- retain an attributable audit record for material scenario, review, wind-tunnel, implication, signpost, and observation commands.

## Collective-intelligence safeguards

- scenario narratives remain human-authored interpretations;
- reviews are attributable and updateable by the reviewer, not anonymous votes detached from accountability;
- aggregate review scores retain the number of reviewers and confidence range;
- wind-tunnel judgements do not alter decision options, advance the decision lifecycle, or select an outcome;
- signpost observations do not automatically declare a scenario “true” or trigger organisational action;
- all cross-record references are constrained to the same organisation, canvas, scenario set, or linked decision as appropriate;
- archived canvases and scenario sets are read-only;
- record creators, accountable owners, and organisation managers retain governed editing rights.

## Deliberately excluded

Phase 13 does not provide probabilistic forecasting, automated scenario generation, portfolio optimisation, automatic strategy selection, causal simulation, or unrestricted whiteboarding. Forecasting and portfolio-level adaptive strategy require later complete vertical slices with their own evidence, calibration, and governance controls.
