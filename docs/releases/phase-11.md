# Phase 11 release — evidence, sources, and signal intelligence

Phase 11 begins the foresight-native CrowdSmarter workflow. Organisations can now preserve what they observed, assess its credibility and future significance, monitor strategic concerns, and connect emerging change to accountable decisions.

## Source intelligence

- first-class sources with author, publisher, publication date, URL or internal reference;
- source type, credibility assessment, rationale, status, and supersession;
- private source attachments with generated storage paths, size limits, file-signature checks, and SHA-256 digests;
- tenant-authorised, audited private downloads;
- structured sources available to decision evidence;
- source metadata and available files included in customer exports.

## Signal intelligence

- human-created STEEP signals;
- current observation and future implication kept separate;
- near-, medium-, and long-term horizons;
- weak-signal, emerging-pattern, and established-trend maturity;
- opportunity, threat, both, or unclear polarity;
- explicit impact and uncertainty assessments;
- accountable owners and review states;
- strategic watchlists;
- explicit signal-to-decision relevance links;
- foresight radar, search integration, decision overview, audit, and offboarding safeguards.

## Zero-cost public-feed ingestion

- manually configured RSS and Atom subscriptions;
- safe conditional synchronisation using ETag and Last-Modified;
- private/local-address, redirect, unsafe XML, response-size, and timeout protections;
- production domain allowlist;
- feed items imported as unassessed sources only;
- no automatic signal interpretation and no paid content provider.

## Product boundary

Phase 11 does not yet model trends, drivers, causal systems, futures wheels, scenarios, wind-tunnelling, forecasting, or adaptive signposts. Those require distinct human-governed domain slices and remain the next foresight phases.
