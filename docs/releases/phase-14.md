# Phase 14 release notes — collective evaluation and prioritisation

Phase 14 adds governed methods for turning diverse independent judgements into transparent decision support. It deliberately separates contribution, aggregation, recommendation, and accountable authority.

## Customer capabilities

- create decision-linked evaluation exercises using a multi-criteria scorecard, approval voting, consent and objections, or iterative Delphi rounds;
- configure accountable ownership, optional peer anonymity, sealed results, quorum, approval thresholds, and objection thresholds;
- define weighted criteria with explicit scales and direction;
- collect one confidence-rated submission per participant and round;
- preserve independent judgement by hiding peer contributions and aggregates until a round closes;
- show quorum, threshold outcomes, confidence dispersion, weighted option rankings, and criterion-weight sensitivity;
- publish facilitator feedback between Delphi rounds without editing participant submissions;
- preserve attributable minority reports and alternative recommendations;
- create organisation-level prioritisation portfolios with optional budget and capacity envelopes;
- add candidate decisions, weighted portfolio criteria, mandatory candidates, independent assessments, and authority selections;
- receive a transparent constrained recommendation with resource totals and exclusion reasons after assessment closes;
- search, audit, export, and protect ownership for all Phase 14 records.

## Collective-intelligence safeguards

- anonymity is peer-facing, not a loss of system accountability;
- blind rounds protect independence from anchoring and popularity effects;
- method-specific validation preserves the meaning of approval and consent;
- uncertainty and disagreement are displayed rather than averaged away;
- minority analysis remains first-class and exportable;
- portfolio recommendations never select, fund, finalise, or transition a decision;
- managerial actions and collective assessments remain separate records with separate rationale.

## Deliberately excluded

Phase 14 does not provide probabilistic forecasting, forecast calibration, quadratic voting, liquid democracy, automated consensus, mixed-integer optimisation, automatic resource allocation, or automated decision finalisation. Forecasting, trigger rules, backcasting, and adaptive action portfolios belong to Phase 15.
