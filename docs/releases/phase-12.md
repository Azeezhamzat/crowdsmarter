# Phase 12 release notes — foresight canvas and systems mapping

Phase 12 turns Phase 11 signal intelligence into structured systems interpretation. It does not predict one future or create decisions automatically.

## Customer capabilities

- create bounded foresight canvases around a focal question and horizon year;
- synthesise signals into trends, drivers, critical uncertainties, predetermined elements, and wild cards;
- assess driver impact, uncertainty, direction, STEEP category, and accountable ownership;
- preserve an explicit rationale when a signal is linked to a driver;
- map stakeholders by role, interests, influence, exposure, and current stance in an influence–exposure matrix;
- record directed causal relationships with polarity, strength, delay, and rationale;
- identify reinforcing, balancing, mixed, or uncertain feedback loops grounded in an explicitly ordered sequence of at least two drivers;
- explore first-, second-, and third-order consequences with a Futures Wheel;
- place current-system pressures, transition activity, and emerging futures in Three Horizons;
- convert analysis into owned strategic implications and connect them to live decisions;
- show linked implications in the decision executive overview;
- find canvases, drivers, feedback loops, and implications through tenant-safe PostgreSQL search;
- include all Phase 12 records in customer-controlled exports and the audit trail.

## Governance boundaries

- viewers remain read-only;
- contributors may create mapping records;
- record creators, accountable owners, and organisation managers may edit governed records;
- archived canvases and their contents are read-only;
- all linked signals, drivers, decisions, and owners must share the same organisation;
- feedback loops and causal links are human interpretations, not automatically inferred facts;
- AI does not create, connect, score, or approve system relationships.

## Deliberately excluded

Phase 12 does not yet provide scenario construction, wind-tunnelling, forecasting, portfolio optimisation, or an unrestricted whiteboard. Those belong to later complete vertical slices.
