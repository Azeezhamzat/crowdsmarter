The missing connection between an open call and an accountable award.

In most grant programmes, the call, community input, scoring, approval, disbursement, and reporting live in separate tools — or no tool at all. CrowdSmarter keeps every stage connected, so the reasoning behind a funding decision is still there when a board member, an auditor, or a declined applicant asks why.

Participatory by design

Evidence tied to every application

Scoring with visible disagreement

Why CrowdSmarter runs it this way

Built by people who think in systems, futures, and collective judgement — not just software.

Grantmaking gets treated like a series of separate transactions: one application, one score, one email. We don't build it that way, because that's not what it actually is.

Systems thinking

A grant round isn't a transaction — it's one system connecting your funding pool, your applicants, and the community they serve. Rounds are designed so feedback flows back into eligibility and criteria, not just into a closed file.

Futures thinking

Rounds get designed around where the need is heading, not just what was fundable last year — using the same scenario and signal tools built for strategic foresight work, applied to who you fund next.

Collective intelligence

Structuring many honest voices — staff, board, community reviewers — into one defensible decision, without averaging away the disagreement that actually matters.

Designed for organisational trust

Your award records should stay inspectable, portable, and under human authority.

Your organisation keeps authority over every award, exports its own records at any time, and can inspect the full history behind each decision. Community applicants join with just a name and email — no account, no password, nothing else collected.
